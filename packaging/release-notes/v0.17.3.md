## Features

* mac: updates ask for keychain access once instead of once per saved login
* mac: the app keeps the spotify icon after an update
* closing the window quits the app by default instead of hiding it in the tray
* mixed playback is on by default
* clicking the player bar no longer switches the visualizer unless you turn it on in settings
* local songs missing from your folders are greyed out and skipped, with no error message
* the sidebar hides empty liked songs and local files right after sign-in
* fixes for the hazy background showing the wrong cover or an unblurred one
