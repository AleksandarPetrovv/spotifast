## Features

* download songs, albums and playlists as mp3, wav, ogg or flac
* add songs, playlists and mixes from youtube or soundcloud into your playlists, with editable titles and artists
* add songs from your pc into your playlists, with their covers
* remove local songs from playlists
* romanization for japanese, chinese and korean lyrics
* musixmatch, netease, lrclib and genius as fallback lyrics providers, with stricter matching
* pick your own emoji font (like apple's) in settings
* caches your playlists so they show instantly on startup
* plays local files from the folders spotify desktop uses
* updates come from this fork

yt-dlp, ffmpeg and deno are used if you have them, otherwise spotifast downloads them into its own folder the first time they are needed.
