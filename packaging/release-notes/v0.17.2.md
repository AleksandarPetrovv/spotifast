## Features

* bump to [v0.17.1](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/releases/tag/v0.17.1) and [v0.17.2](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/releases/tag/v0.17.2)
* mixed playback: soloist plays spotify songs and the built-in player plays local files, in one queue
* hazy theme: the playing cover blurred behind everything, with an edit theme popup
* lyrics plus look for lyrics in the hazy theme
* lyrics open full screen inside the window, above the player bar, and scroll smoothly between lines
* local songs of the playing playlist show in the queue during mixed playback
* listening activity moved to the top bar, beside settings
* the app opens straight into your library while soloist starts
* the sidebar hides empty liked songs and local files, and the filters fold behind a button
* the playing playlist stays when a song arrives without its context
* the window keeps at least its minimum size on start
* fixes for seeking, the progress bar, the window title while paused and flashes between songs
* a restored song plays from the position the bar shows
