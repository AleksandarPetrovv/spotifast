## Features

* bump to [v0.15.0](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/releases/tag/v0.15.0), [v0.16.0](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/releases/tag/v0.16.0) and [v0.17.0](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/releases/tag/v0.17.0)
* windows media player skins, with previews of every skin in settings
* spotify soloist as a player, with its own setup in settings
* the playing playlist is remembered across a restart, so play and repeat pick up the playlist instead of looping one song
* shuffle and repeat show a dot under them when they're on
* local song names show spaces and accents properly
* paste a spotify link in the search bar to open it, shift+q queues the selected songs and shift+n plays them next
